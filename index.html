About

I'm a third-year PhD candidate in the Computational Linguistics Lab at Fictional University, advised by Prof. J. Alvarez. My research sits at the intersection of multilingual NLP and model interpretability — I'm interested in why large language models generalize across languages the way they do, and how we can make that behavior more predictable and efficient.

Before my PhD, I completed my MSc in Computer Science at Riverside Institute of Technology, where I worked on low-resource machine translation. My work has been supported by a Graduate Research Fellowship, and I've spent time as a research intern at two industry labs.
